“The Live Ceremony” is the source phase label. A general catalogue can display “Live interaction” while preserving the source mapping. A human moment does not require a DTG multi-task ceremony.

## Use the other inputs as coverage checks

The reference image reviewed in the source study contributes eight interaction structures: connecting, negotiated consent, qualifying, delegation, subscription, accumulation, lifecycle and adjudication. They broaden coverage beyond credential presentation; they do not form eight additional moments.

The First Person white paper adds discovery and connection, wallets and credentials, payments and value, secure communications and signing, and multi-party work. The community-anchored proof proposal adds concrete evidence, freshness and privacy considerations. These are functional domains and technical constraints. [A9]({{ '/docs/references/#a9' | relative_url }}), [A12]({{ '/docs/references/#a12' | relative_url }})

## Treat threats as dimensions across the catalogue

Impersonation can affect an invitation, a live request, recovery or delegated authority. Keep fraud and abuse resistance as a family and attach threat tags across the catalogue. A suspicious situation does not, by itself, justify a fourteenth universal moment.

Use role, channel, lifecycle, evidence and consequence as additional filters. Record “not applicable” where a product does not encounter a moment. The 13 moments and 18 families are proposed coverage structures, not proofs of completeness.
