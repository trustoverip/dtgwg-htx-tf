# Design for human trust

A practical starting point for discussing trust and designing interactions that help people understand, decide and recover.

## The HTX mission

The HTX Task Force designs the conditions, patterns and practices from which a healthy and safe human trust experience can emerge and be operated.

## How to use this material

1. Use the taxonomy to distinguish a moment, task, ceremony and pattern.
2. Choose a concrete human problem and locate its family.
3. Complete the template, prototype material states and record evaluation findings.

The 18 families are an organizing index, not 18 finished patterns or required screens. Publication, implementation and evaluation are recorded separately.

HDX is the catalogue label for human experience design in digital trust. The community name remains **Human Trust Experience (HTX)**. This contribution is proposed guidance; publication here does not establish community endorsement.
