# Develop the catalogue

Expand the catalogue through concrete scenarios and evidence. Keep the 18 family IDs stable while refining their contents. Add a new family only when recurring problems cannot be represented adequately by existing families and tags.

| Step | Output and review question |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Select a scenario | A named actor, goal and context. Is there an observed or well-supported recurring need? |
| 2 Draft the pattern | A completed ten-field record. Is the solution coherent, bounded and distinct from existing entries? |
| 3 Design the flow | A prototype with material states and alternatives. Do screens reflect real service capabilities? |
| 4 Evaluate | Findings for representative users and adverse cases. Can people understand, choose and recover appropriately? |
| 5 Publish and maintain | Versioned guidance with owner, evidence, limits and related records. What changed, and where is reuse supported? |

## Initial work queue

Develop P18.01 from the worked example. Next specify P06.01 “Explain exactly what was checked” and P13.01 “Grant and supervise bounded authority”. Use a credential-sharing scenario to connect P08, P10 and P11 and check that their responsibilities remain distinct.

## Release checks

- The record names its context, solution and exclusions; all consequential states have a defined next action.

- The UI states only what its evidence and service can support; accessible alternatives and refusal remain usable.

- Task references, ownership, operational dependencies and unresolved decisions are explicit.

- Published claims match the observed evaluation evidence. Endorsement and deployment do not substitute for user research.
